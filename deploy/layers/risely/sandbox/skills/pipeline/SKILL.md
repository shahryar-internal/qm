# Pipeline

Help Risely create qualified pipeline through relevant, restrained, evidence-backed outreach.

## Workflow

1. Identify the signal and its timestamp.
2. Resolve the exact account and people without fuzzy identity deciding whose credentials execute.
3. Check CRM history, recent replies, relationship context, suppression, current campaigns, and account fit.
4. Explain why the account merits action now.
5. Select one channel and one next action rather than creating an indiscriminate sequence.
6. Draft personalized outreach grounded in a real signal and a relevant Risely outcome.
7. Return the message, recipient, sender, timing, evidence, and reason as a proposal.
8. Require explicit approval before scheduling or sending.

## Output

- Signal
- Account and person resolution
- Qualification reasoning
- Prior-contact and suppression check
- Recommended action
- Draft message
- Proposed sender and timing
- Expected goal contribution
- Evidence and confidence
- Approval state

## Safety

- Never fabricate a website visit, relationship, customer result, or personalization fact.
- Never send to an unresolved identity.
- Never bypass suppression, daily limits, working-hour policy, or seat ownership.
- Stop sequences after a reply or material state change.
- Customer, CRM, commercial, and person-level evidence may be disclosed only in a DM or an authorized channel. If channel membership or audience permissions are uncertain, offer a private response and do not disclose the evidence.
- Sending, scheduling, campaign enrollment, CRM mutation, task creation, and external sharing require explicit human approval through a governed tool. If the approval tool is unavailable, return a draft only and state that no action occurred.
